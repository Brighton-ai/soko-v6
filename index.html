Fee collection

The bursar's office, automated

Most schools don't lose fees to fraud. They lose them to follow-up that never happens. Shule does the following up.

  • Bulk-generate a whole term. Build the fee structure once per class, then raise every invoice for the term in a single action.
  • M-Pesa payments match themselves. Payments land against the right invoice and the balance updates the moment the parent pays.
  • Three reminders, sent without you. A nightly job finds every overdue invoice and chases it up to three times, counting each attempt.
  • Every payment posts to the ledger. Fees hit the general ledger as a balanced double entry, so your books are always closable.
  • Bursaries handled properly. Waivers go through request and approval, so nobody quietly writes off a term.

Questions

The things heads ask first

How long does setup take?

Most schools are live in under two weeks. Send us your student register in whatever form it exists — a spreadsheet, an export from your old system — and we map and import it. Fee structures and grading bands are configured with you in one session.

What if a parent has no smartphone?

Everything critical works over SMS. Fee reminders, balances, absence alerts and results notifications are sent as text messages. The guardian portal link opens in any mobile browser and needs no app or account.

Do we have to move our bank or paybill?

No. Shule connects to your existing paybill or till and reconciles payments against invoices. Funds settle to your account exactly as they do today.

Does it handle CBC as well as 8-4-4?

Grading scales are fully configurable, so both run side by side. You define the bands — grade, score range, points and remark — and the system applies them. Full CBC strand and sub-strand assessment is on the near-term roadmap.

Who owns the data?

The school does. You can export students, fees, results and ledger entries at any time in standard formats. If you leave, you leave with everything.

What happens when a teacher leaves mid-term?

Marks and attendance already entered stay attached to the record with the original entry name against them. Reassign the subject to another teacher and the class carries on. Nothing is lost with the person.
